Dataset Quality Check

Not all public data is research-grade. Use this checklist to critically evaluate your chosen dataset before committing to it.

The "Goldilocks" Dataset

A perfect dataset rarely exists. Your goal isn't perfection, but determining if the flaws are manageable. If you answered "No" to Provenance or Documentation, heavily reconsider using the dataset.

Pro Tip

Always check the methodology section to understand how the data was collected. Bias often hides in the sampling method.

05
Stage 05

Analysis Decision Guide

Determine the correct statistical or analytical approach based on the types of variables you identified in Stage 03.

Compare Groups

Looking for differences between categories.

Recommended Tests:

  • • T-Test (2 groups)
  • • ANOVA (3+ groups)

Find Relationships

How two continuous variables move together.

Recommended Tests:

  • • Pearson Correlation
  • • Spearman Rank

Predict Outcomes

Using variables to predict a specific result.

Recommended Tests:

  • • Linear Regression
  • • Logistic Regression

Note on assumptions: Every statistical test assumes certain things about your data (e.g., normal distribution, equal variance). Always verify these assumptions in your software (SPSS, R, Python) before finalizing results.

AI Prompt Toolkit

Leverage LLMs (like ChatGPT or Claude) to refine your methodology. Use these engineered prompts to stress-test your research design.

Critique My Question

"Act as a strict university data science professor. I am investigating [Topic]. My independent variable is [IV] and dependent variable is [DV]. My research question is: '[Paste Question]'. Critique this question for specificity, measurability, and potential confounding variables. Suggest 2 alternative formulations."

Data Cleaning Guide

"I am working with a public dataset from [Source] about [Topic]. I have identified missing values in my [Variable Name] column, which is a [Data Type] variable. What are the statistically rigorous ways to handle this missing data? Explain the pros and cons of imputation versus exclusion in this specific context."
